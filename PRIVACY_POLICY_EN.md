## 1. Controller and scope

The data controller for the bot is **Mattia Dossena**. Privacy questions and requests may be sent to **dob.dossenixbots@gmail.com**.

This policy covers data processed by the bot for simulated government and justice activities in a Discord roleplay community, including data entered by authorized operators about other players. It does not replace Discord's privacy policy or policies covering other server activities.

## 2. Data processed and sources

Depending on the features used, the bot processes:

- Discord user, server, role, channel, message and interaction IDs, display names, and links between accounts and characters;
- command fields, forms, content required for text commands, and message references used for publishing and recovery;
- in-game character data, including name, surname, fictional date of birth, Citizen ID, images, positions, status, notes and warnings;
- roleplay documents and records, descriptions of events, participants, in-game attachments and evidence, virtual amounts, compensation and operation history;
- technical metadata, errors, dated change records identifying the operator, and database backups;
- email addresses and the content of requests sent to the privacy contact.

Data comes from Discord's APIs, users and authorized operators. The bot uses message content access to provide its stated functions, but does not generally archive the server's complete message history.

Identity, court and payment fields are intended only for fiction. Users must not enter real identity documents, tax IDs, health information, criminal or court data, or financial information. In-game information linked to an identifiable Discord account may still be personal data.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

Data is processed to provide requested features, check permissions, attribute actions to authorized users, keep consistent in-game records, prevent abuse, resolve errors and answer data subject requests.

For authorized operators who voluntarily use the bot, processing is necessary to provide the requested service under these Terms (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). For information entered about other players, and for security, audit and service continuity, the basis is the legitimate interest in organizing roleplay and protecting the bot (Article 6(1)(f)), only where necessary and after considering data subjects' rights and reasonable expectations, particularly for minors. Processing required by law is based on a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).

Required information must be provided for the relevant feature to work; irrelevant optional fields may be left blank. The bot performs game calculations and permission checks, but does not make automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant real-world effects.

## 4. Recipients, hosting and transfers

Data may be viewed by the controller and members authorized through Discord roles and permissions. Documents and attachments are posted in configured operational, personal or archive channels; anyone with access to those channels may see them. Operators must select channels with appropriate permissions.

The bot uses:

- **Discord** for APIs, accounts, servers, messages and attachments, under [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy);
- **Wispbyte Premium** to run the bot and store its files and database. Wispbyte's commercial page lists Canada for bot hosting; the technical location may depend on the assigned node;
- **Google Gmail** to receive and manage messages sent to the contact address, under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Transfers are handled under the mechanisms made available by the relevant provider and applicable law. Data subjects may contact the controller for current information about the hosting node and available safeguards.

Data is not sold, used for personalized advertising or used to train artificial intelligence models.

## 5. Retention

Data is kept only for as long as needed for the relevant purpose:

| Category | Period or criterion |
| --- | --- |
| Attachments archived by the Government module | Deleted after 10 days. Checks run every 10 minutes while the bot is online; outages or permission errors may cause technical delays. |
| Active character records, photos and related evidence | Kept while the character or record remains active and needed for roleplay. Inactive items are reviewed at least every 90 days and deleted when no longer needed. |
| Documents, services, compensation and game history | Kept during the relevant season or operational cycle and for up to 12 months after it ends, unless deleted earlier when no longer needed. |
| Audit records | Kept for up to 12 months for security, disputes and operation integrity. Unnecessary information is minimized. |
| Automatic backups | Normally created every 24 hours and limited to the 14 most recent copies. Deleted live data disappears as backups rotate, normally within 14 backup cycles. |
| Technical logs | Size-based rotation keeps one current file and up to five archives of about 5 MiB each. Logs are reviewed and deleted when no longer needed for troubleshooting or security. |
| Email requests | Kept as needed to handle the request and normally no longer than 12 months after closure, unless required for legal protection or compliance. |

Expiry, revocation or closure of an in-game document and report resets do not automatically erase data. Copies downloaded or forwarded by other users are outside the bot's control.

If the bot permanently ceases operation, stored Discord API data and other personal data will be deleted promptly unless retention is required by law.

## 6. Rights and deletion

To request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability where applicable, email **dob.dossenixbots@gmail.com** and provide your Discord user ID and request. Do not send passwords or identity documents initially; any identity verification will be proportionate.

Requests are normally handled within one month. Where granted, deletion covers data under the controller's control in the live database, bot-managed messages and attachments, followed by removal from backups through rotation. Strictly necessary audit information may be restricted or pseudonymized instead of immediately deleted where needed to protect the service or record how a request was handled. Any applicable limitation will be explained.

Data subjects may object to legitimate-interest processing and lodge a complaint with the [Italian Data Protection Authority](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/) or their competent supervisory authority.

## 7. Security and minors

The bot uses roles and permissions, operation records, separate credentials and backups. Access to the hosting panel and database is limited to authorized persons. Operators must avoid posting data in channels accessible to people who do not need it.

The bot is not intended for anyone below Discord's minimum age in their country. If the controller learns that data belongs to a person who was not permitted to use Discord, it will be deleted as required.

## 8. Changes

Material changes will be announced in the server and reflected by updating the date on this page. For security or privacy reports, contact **dob.dossenixbots@gmail.com**.
